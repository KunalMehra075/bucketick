The honest answers

Questions you were about to ask

Is Bucketick free?

Yes. Everything you need to dream, chase, and remember is free. A premium tier adds AI planning and custom themes later, but the heart of it costs nothing.

Is this just another to-do app?

No, and we will gently push back if you call it that. To-do apps are about clearing things. Bucketick is about collecting them, sharing the journey, and keeping the memory once you are done.

Can I keep some lists private?

Of course. Every list is private by default. You decide what the world sees and what stays just yours.

Can I do this with friends?

That is the best part. Invite people to a shared list, assign nothing, pressure no one, and somehow get more done together than alone.
