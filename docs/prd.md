# Bucketick Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

---

# Product Vision

Bucketick is a social platform where people create bucket lists, pursue meaningful life goals, document their journey, collaborate with others, inspire the community, and celebrate achievements.

Traditional bucket list apps are private checklists.

Bucketick transforms them into living stories.

Instead of simply checking off goals, users build memories, share progress, inspire others, and help one another accomplish dreams.

Bucketick is designed to become the world's largest community centered around dreams, adventures, life experiences, and meaningful achievements.

---

# Mission

Help millions of people stop postponing their dreams.

Provide a beautiful platform where goals become journeys, journeys become memories, and memories inspire others.

---

# Vision Statement

Create the world's largest platform where people dream together, build together, travel together, celebrate together, and inspire future generations.

---

# Core Philosophy

Bucketick is built on five principles.

## 1. Dreams deserve to be remembered

Goals shouldn't disappear after completion.

Every completed goal becomes a memory.

---

## 2. Progress is more inspiring than success

The journey is more valuable than the destination.

Users should share progress, not only completion.

---

## 3. Great experiences happen together

Life is better with people.

Bucketick is designed around collaboration.

Friends.

Families.

Couples.

Teams.

Communities.

---

## 4. Inspiration creates action

Seeing other people's dreams motivates us to pursue our own.

Public bucket lists become inspiration for the community.

---

## 5. Achievements deserve celebration

Every meaningful milestone deserves recognition.

Bucketick celebrates progress through achievements, badges, community appreciation, and memorable timelines.

---

# The Problem

Today's bucket list apps suffer from several issues:

* They are simple checklists.
* They lack community.
* They don't encourage accountability.
* There is little motivation to return.
* Completed goals disappear.
* There is no storytelling.
* There is almost no collaboration.

People have dreams.

Current products don't help them pursue those dreams together.

---

# The Solution

Bucketick combines

* Goal tracking
* Social networking
* Community
* Storytelling
* Collaboration
* Memory preservation
* Inspiration

into one platform.

Users don't simply create goals.

They create journeys.
